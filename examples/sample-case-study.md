# Sample case study (fictional)

Lumen Freight and Tidewater Analytics are invented for this example. The names, quotes and details are made up to show the format.

## Headline
"We stopped finding out about late deliveries from our customers."

## The situation before
Lumen Freight's dispatch team tracked every shipment in a shared spreadsheet. Delays showed up when a customer called to ask where their order was. Priya Rao, head of operations, described mornings spent chasing drivers by phone before she could answer a single customer.

## Why they chose it
Priya wanted one place where the team could see which deliveries were at risk before the customer noticed. She said the deciding factor was that her dispatchers could learn it in an afternoon.

## How they use it
Each morning the dispatch team checks a list of shipments flagged as at risk and calls those drivers first. Priya reviews the list again before lunch.

## The outcome
Last month a flagged delivery let the team reroute a driver and tell the customer about the delay before the customer asked. Priya said customer calls to dispatch have become rarer and calmer.

## Pull quote
"Now my team calls the driver first, and the customer hears from us before they have to ask."
Priya Rao, Head of Operations, Lumen Freight
