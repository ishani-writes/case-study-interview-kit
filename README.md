# Case Study Interview Kit

One customer call in, a case study and a LinkedIn post out, with the customer as the hero.

## The problem

Case studies stall for two reasons. The interview produces vague answers like "it saved us time," and the draft that follows reads like a vendor ad. The customer then has to approve a story that doesn't sound like them, and the project drags on or dies.

## What this does

A set of interview questions that pull out specific, quotable answers, plus two prompts. One drafts the case study from the transcript. The other writes a short LinkedIn post that gives the customer a reason to engage.

## How to use it

1. Ask the questions in `interview-questions.md` and record the call, with the customer's permission.
2. Paste the transcript into `prompts/01-draft-case-study.md` and run it.
3. Send the draft and the claims list to the customer for approval.
4. Once approved, run `prompts/02-linkedin-post.md`.

## What's inside

- `interview-questions.md`: the questions and interview tips
- `prompts/01-draft-case-study.md`: transcript to case study
- `prompts/02-linkedin-post.md`: case study to LinkedIn post
- `examples/sample-case-study.md`: a worked example with a fictional company
