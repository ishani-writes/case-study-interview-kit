# Case Study Interview Kit

One customer interview in, a full case study, an approval checklist and a LinkedIn post out, with the customer as the hero.

## The problem

Case studies stall for two reasons. The interview produces vague answers like "it saved us time," and the draft that follows reads like a vendor ad. The customer then has to approve a story that doesn't sound like them, and the project drags on or dies.

## What this does

A set of interview questions mapped to each part of the story, a template that says what every section must contain, and three prompts: one drafts the case study, one checks every claim against the transcript, and one writes the LinkedIn post.

## The structure

Every case study follows twelve sections: headline and summary, at a glance, the challenge, what they were looking for, why they chose us, what they built, what surprised them, results, what's next, who made it happen, the two "about" blurbs and a call to action. Quotes sit inside the story instead of in a separate box.

## How to use it

1. Get permission to record and to use names and quotes. Collect any usage data the customer has approved.
2. Run the interview with `interview-questions.md`.
3. Paste the transcript into `prompts/01-draft-case-study.md` and run it.
4. Run `prompts/02-claims-check.md` and send the customer the draft with the list of questions.
5. Once the customer approves, run `prompts/03-linkedin-post.md`.

## What's inside

- `interview-questions.md`: questions grouped by section, plus interview tips
- `case-study-template.md`: what each section must contain, with lengths
- `prompts/01-draft-case-study.md`: transcript to case study
- `prompts/02-claims-check.md`: checks every number, quote and claim
- `prompts/03-linkedin-post.md`: approved case study to LinkedIn post
- `examples/sample-case-study.md`: a full worked example with a fictional company
- `examples/sample-linkedin-post.md`: the matching post
