# Interview questions

Record the call, and get written permission to use the customer's name and quotes. Ask for specifics every time. If the answer is "it saved us time," follow up with "Can you give me one example from last week?"

## Before
1. What were you doing before, and what was not working?
2. What did that problem cost you in time, money or stress, in your own words?

## The decision
3. What made you start looking for a solution?
4. What else did you consider, and what tipped the decision?

## Using it
5. Walk me through how your team uses it on a normal day.
6. What surprised you after you started?

## Results
7. What changed for you? Give me one specific example.
8. Is there a number you track that moved? Only use it if they say it and approve it.

## Advice
9. What would you tell someone who is on the fence?
10. Is there anything I should have asked and didn't?

## After the call
Send the customer the draft and the list of claims for approval before anything is published.
