# Interview questions

## Before the call
- Get written permission to record, and to use the customer's name and quotes.
- Ask the customer for any usage data they are happy to share, such as volumes, dates and which features they use.
- Ask who else on their team should join. A second voice often gives the best detail on how the product is used day to day.

## Background
1. Tell me about the company and your role.
2. Who uses it day to day?

## The challenge
3. What was not working?
4. What did it cost you, in time, money or customer impact? Use your own words.
5. What had you already tried?

## What they were looking for
6. When you started looking, what did the ideal solution need to do? If you had to rank your must-haves, what comes first?

## The decision
7. What else did you consider?
8. What tipped the decision?
9. Was there a test or pilot, and what did you see?
10. Who else had to say yes?

## Using it
11. Walk me through a normal week.
12. Which features do you use most, and which have you ignored?
13. Did you build anything you did not plan to?

## Surprises
14. What surprised you, good or bad?
15. What took longer than you expected?

## Results
16. What changed? Give me one specific example.
17. Which numbers do you track, and can we use them?
18. What would you have lost if you had not made the change?

## What's next
19. What are you building next?
20. What does this make possible that was not possible before?

## Who made it happen
21. Who on your team did the work?
22. How did our team work with yours? What would you want us to do differently?

## Advice
23. What would you tell a peer who is on the fence?
24. Is there anything I should have asked and did not?

## Tips
- Ask for a specific example every time. If the answer is "it saved us time," follow up with "Can you give me one example from last week?"
- Leave a pause after an answer. People often add the most useful detail when the room goes quiet.
- Ask the advice question last. The answer is usually the best quote you'll get.
- After the call, send the customer the draft and a list of the claims in it. Nothing is published until they approve.
